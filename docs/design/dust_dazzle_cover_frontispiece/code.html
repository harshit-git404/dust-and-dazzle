Curator’s Foreword

About This Collection

Forty years of memory bridging the mud-brick verandas of Purvanchal to the neon thoroughfares of Delhi. This volume gathers intimate chronicles of departure and return—a ledger of sun-baked courtyards, monsoons recorded in clay jugs, and the quiet loneliness of high-rise balconies under amber streetlamps.

Written across four decades on loose vellum notebooks and later keyed under the hum of a single incandescent desk lamp in Daryaganj, these tales do not seek to reconcile the rural with the urban. Instead, they let both live in delicate tension. The dust of the village path settles gently on the leather soles of the commuter; the dazzle of the metropolitan skyline dissolves into the amber flame of a mustard-oil diya.

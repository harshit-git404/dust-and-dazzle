About this Collection

Spanning four turbulent yet tender decades, Dust and Dazzle brings together thirteen intimate short memoirs tracing the trajectory from the clay threshing floors of eastern Uttar Pradesh to the crowded bookstalls of Daryaganj.

Each tale acts as an unhurried window into an India undergoing seismic transformation—marked by the scent of monsoon earth, creaking well pulleys, and the sharp hiss of evening steam engines at Old Delhi junction.
